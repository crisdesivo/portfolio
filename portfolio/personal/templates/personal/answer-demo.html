§ A.1How it works

Question

You type anything, in your own words.

Selection

The model reads the approved question-and-answer pairs. Its output is constrained to one of them, plus a yes/no: does it really answer you? If not, nothing is shown.

Answer

The chosen answer is shown exactly as written. Nothing is generated.

Why am I still getting wrong answers?

This demo runs a 1.5-billion-parameter model so it can be hosted cheaply; it sometimes picks a less fitting answer. A larger model, such as gpt-4o-mini, picks reliably.

Where is this useful?

Anywhere a wrong answer is costly: a business website's assistant, support for a product, guidance through a process. The answers stay yours.
